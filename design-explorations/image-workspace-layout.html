What works

Image grows from ~344 px to ~680 px with no new interaction: same panels, same sticky prompt, same order. The fix is mostly a grid ratio in GenerationWorkspaceLayout plus a below-image action row in ResultStack.

What risks

The 40% setup column still scrolls on a short laptop, and older results still stack below the fold. It solves size, not iteration.

Pick this if

you want L1 fixed this week with no new UI concepts to learn.

What works

The newest result gets the stage (~620 px) with its actions beside it, and the filmstrip makes earlier runs reachable without scrolling. The rail keeps prompt and Generate together and states each setting in one line.

Comparing two results means switching the stage back and forth. The filmstrip needs a selected-result state that does not exist today.

Pick this if

single-image quality matters most and you want a calm, focused canvas.

What works

The model list, the choice that changes price and quality most, stays one glance away with its prices, which answers B's biggest risk. Shape and size, which rarely change between runs, stay folded. Results still get about 420 px each, 2-up.

What risks

Two layouts to maintain: the rail at desktop, the chip below 1024 px. Engines with long model lists (Kie, fal, Runware) need the rail to scroll, and their extra controls still need a home in the chip row.

Pick this if

you like B's page but comparing models by price is part of how people use the studio.

What works

Moving the engine into the rail frees the full-width engine row, so the newest result reaches about 650 px with its actions beside it, and earlier runs sit one row below. Each model row gives price plus capability tags from catalog data (4K, Search, 1K only), and a retired model points at its successor.

What risks

Engines are a menu now, so "two free engines" is a label rather than visible pills, which cuts into the first-run free path from H1. Comparing two results at full size means clicking between them. On a 900 px-tall screen the history strip starts below the fold; capping the newest result at about 55vh keeps it in view.

Pick this if

you want E's clarity about models with C's focus on the newest result.
